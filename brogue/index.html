BROGUE

Dive into the Dungeons of Doom for the Amulet of Yendor, 26 levels down. Brogue: Community Edition, the 2009 roguelike by Brian Walker.

Tap a spot to walk there, and tap things to use them. Hold on anything to see what it is. Pinch to zoom and drag to look around. The bar has Escape, Enter and a keyboard for Brogue's letter keys. Turn your phone sideways for a bigger view.

Saves stay on this device. Brogue CE is free software under the GNU AGPL v3: this port's source and Brogue CE 1.15.1's.
